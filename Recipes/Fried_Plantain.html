Fried Plantain

Description

A popular dish made from plantain sliced and fried until crispy on the outside and soft and sweet on the inside

Ingredients

  • Plantains
  • Salt
  • Cooking Oil
  • Spices

Instructions

  1. Slice plantains
  2. Salt plantains
  3. Spice plantains
  4. Place cooking oil in pot and heat
  5. Poor plantains into pot
  6. Mix plaintains every 30 seconds till golden brown
  7. Scoop plantians out and enjoy
