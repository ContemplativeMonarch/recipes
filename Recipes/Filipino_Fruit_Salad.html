Filipino Fruit Salad

Description

A creamy, sweet, and colorful dessert made from a mix of fruits and a luscious dressing, typically served chilled

Ingredients

  • Sweetened condensed milk
  • All purpose cream
  • Fruit cocktail - syrup drained
  • Young coconut meat
  • Kaong/ sugar palm fruit
  • Nata de coco/coconut gel
  • Cup cheese

Instructions

  1. Mix the sweetened condensed milk and all purpose cream in a container with a lid
  2. Add the rest of the ingredients and toss them
  3. Cover the bowl with cling wrap and chill for several hours before serving
